## 5. Value Proposition Identification

Eight genuinely distinct differentiators found. Environmental variables (bedroom temperature, light, noise) are folded into #6 rather than listed separately because they are one input into the same engine.

| # | Differentiator | What it is | Emotional benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sells subtraction | The only wellness product whose scoreboard (stack count, monthly cost) improves by going DOWN | Permission to stop, relief from overwhelm |
| 2 | Controlled within-subject experiments | 3-on / 3-off alternating blocks, one variable, on your own body, not population averages | Trust, certainty that the answer is about you |
| 3 | Pre-committed metric and honest verdicts | Outcome metric and threshold fixed before the test starts. "Inconclusive" is a real answer with the number shown | Confidence that nobody is fishing or spinning |
| 4 | Pay-nothing guarantee | 60 days, three experiments. If nothing measurably moves, no charge, and you're told before the charge date | Zero risk, control |
| 5 | One tap a day, zero logging | Daily instruction is usually a skip, not an add. Missed taps are unknown, never failed | Ease, no guilt, nothing new to learn |
| 6 | Engine picks the test, you veto | Reads your inventory, your data history, the literature, and environmental inputs, then ranks what's most likely useless. You never decide what to test | Decisions removed, still in control |
| 7 | Runs on data you already have | Months or years of Apple Health history are read during onboarding, so the first analysis is ready before you finish | Instant payoff, no waiting, no new hardware |
| 8 | A compounding personal spec | Two earned lists: what you cut, and the short set of conditions your body runs best under | Identity shift: from follower to authority on yourself |
