# The Distill story

Veer's words, 11 October 2026. The landing page, the pitch and every screen build from this.

I grew up between the art, culture and beaches of Sydney, then moved to Silicon Valley, the centre of tech. Then I spent a year alone in Europe and New York, ambitious, and just starting to meditate. Watching how different cultures live, and how social media shapes all of them, changed how I think.

Wanting to be better is good: how you sleep, how you look, how you feel, the people you spend your days with, the places you go. That's wellness: the whole human experience. But somewhere it tipped into over-optimizing: more metrics, more products, more protocols, until there's no room left to just live it. And modern life got clean and efficient and lost its detail, while people are clearly hungry for the past and for each other again.

Distill keeps what works for you and clears the rest. It learns who you are at your best and helps you become more of that, with real proof: money back, things let go, and knowing yourself.

Less noise. More you.
