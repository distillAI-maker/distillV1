### What changed from v2, and why

- **The "a third of tracker owners quit within six months" line is gone.** It traces to a 2014 consultancy white paper about first-generation trackers, the original is no longer online, and no solid newer figure exists. It should not be stated as a current fact.
- **Two sourced figures replace it.** 60.2% of US adults took a supplement in the past 30 days (CDC/NCHS Data Brief 561, May 2026, NHANES 2021 to 2023). Median monthly spend among supplement users was $50 (CRN/Ipsos consumer survey, 2024; self-reported, so the page says "typical" and "around"). Both sources are named in the page footer.
- "Picks the one habit most likely doing nothing" became "picks the one habit your data can test most clearly", which is what the routing table actually does.
- "Two years of history in Apple Health" became "months of history on your phone", so it is true for more readers.
