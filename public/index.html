    Questions people ask before they connect

    What does it cost?

    The stack audit is free and there's nothing to buy. Distill itself is $20 a month, and you pay nothing unless at least one of your first three experiments measurably moves your sleep or recovery. No card until your first verdict lands.

    What do I get in the free stack audit?

    Your stack counted and costed, the habits your data is most sensitive to, and the first experiment we'd run. It takes under four minutes and there's nothing to buy.

    What does "measurable" actually mean?

    The on-days and off-days have to differ by more than your own day-to-day noise for that metric. Roughly, a difference you'd see 1 in 20 times by chance. The threshold is written down before the experiment starts and it doesn't move. We show the number either way.

    Is this a medical product?

    No. Distill works on sleep, recovery, energy and habits, using goals you set and data you own. "Your sleep improved 22 minutes on nights without the 3pm coffee" is what we say. "This will fix your insomnia" is something we never say. If your question is driven by a medical condition, talk to a clinician, not an app.

    Which wearables work?

    Anything that writes to Apple Health: Apple Watch, Oura, Garmin, Fitbit, WHOOP and most of the category. What matters is a passive data stream, not a brand. If you've got a device with months of history you've never used, that history is the whole point.

    What happens if a verdict is inconclusive?

    You see the number, the size of your normal range, and the word "inconclusive". That's a real answer: for that habit, at that dose, your body didn't show a detectable effect. It goes in your file and the next experiment begins. If all three inside 60 days come back inconclusive, you aren't charged.

    Do I have to log anything?

    One tap a day to confirm you did or didn't do the instruction. No meals, no moods, no journaling. A missed tap is recorded as unknown, never as a failure, and it just makes that day count for less.
