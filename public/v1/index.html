    Before you join

    What can it test?

    Things that can change your sleep within a night or two: coffee and alcohol timing, late workouts, an evening sauna, melatonin, screens in bed. Things your ring can't measure, like skincare or a membership, get checked on day one for cost, use and overlap.

    Do I stop everything at once?

    No. You test one thing at a time for 14 nights. Everything else stays the same until it's that thing's turn.

    What if I miss a day?

    That night counts as unknown, not as a fail. A test can handle a few of those.

    Which devices work?

    Oura, Apple Watch through Apple Health, and Garmin. WHOOP isn't supported yet.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. Distill looks at sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. It never diagnoses or treats anything, and whatever your clinician gave you stays untouched.

    Who isn't it for?

    Anyone without a wearable, anyone training for a specific event, and anyone whose question is medical.
